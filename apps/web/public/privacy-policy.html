        MCP CONNECTIONS AND EXTERNAL AI SERVICES

        This section explains how this Privacy Notice applies when you connect an external AI service, such as ChatGPT or Claude, to MeadTools through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

        Information used and shared

        MeadTools uses your account, access status and connection permissions to authenticate and authorize requests from the service you connect. Basic tools provide brewing calculations, unsaved recipe drafts, and catalog and wiki lookup. Public recipe and brew tools return information from publicly shared records.

        If you explicitly allow account reads, the connected service can request your owned recipes and brews, including private recipes, brewing timelines and notes, recorded readings, and sensor history or summaries. MeadTools sends the requested tool results to that service so it can respond to you. Account-read permission applies to the connection until it expires or you revoke it; it is not a separate confirmation for each record request. Basic access alone does not authorize private account reads.

        The connected AI service receives and processes the results under its own privacy policy and settings. MeadTools does not control how long that service retains the information or how it uses the information after receipt. MeadTools' hosting and database service providers process connection and usage records to operate the integration.

        Connection and usage records

        MeadTools stores connection labels, registered client names and callback addresses, account identifiers, granted permissions, authorization and credential hashes, and connection creation, expiry, revocation and use times. These records support sign-in, permission checks, token rotation, revocation and abuse prevention.

        The MCP usage recorder stores your account identifier, tool name, result category, execution duration and request time for service operation and rate limiting. This usage recorder does not store tool arguments, tool results, passwords or raw access credentials. Your recipes and brewing records remain subject to the other sections of this Privacy Notice.

        Retention and deletion

        We keep MCP authorization and usage records for as long as your MeadTools account exists, so we can operate connections, enforce permissions, and investigate security or abuse issues. Expiring or revoking a connection stops its further authorized use but does not delete its stored history, and deactivating an account does not by itself erase these records.

        If you ask us to delete your account or these records, we will delete them within 30 days of verifying your request, except information we must keep to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent abuse. Copies held in backup archives are handled as described in How long do we keep your information? until they can be deleted.

        You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting contact@meadtools.com or using the MeadTools contact page. Requests are subject to the rights, verification and exceptions described in this Privacy Notice.

        Your connection controls

        You can review and revoke your connections on the MCP setup page. To change a connection's permissions, revoke it and reconnect with your chosen permissions. Revoking a MeadTools connection does not delete information already received by the connected AI service; use that service's privacy controls or contact it about copies it holds.
